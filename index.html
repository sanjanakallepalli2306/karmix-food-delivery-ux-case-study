01 — USER RESEARCH

Designing around real user needs

The research phase focused on common food-ordering frustrations and the moments where users need the most clarity.

🔎

Discovery

Users want fast search, clear categories, useful filters and relevant restaurant suggestions.

🧾

Decision making

Ratings, delivery time, price and food details help users feel confident before adding an item.

⚡

Checkout

A simple cart, visible address and payment summary reduce unnecessary steps before ordering.
